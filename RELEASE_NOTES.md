# GrandHonor 2.5.3

- **Safer:** programs you assign to the Copilot or YOYO key, the screen snip and the event log now open with your own rights, never with GrandHonor's administrator rights. Settings and logs can no longer be redirected elsewhere, and the stored previous version is kept in a protected folder.
- **Setup no longer gets stuck** on "could not close applications": it ends a GrandHonor that hangs, and GrandHonor itself always closes within a few seconds.
- **Warmth slider:** drag the knob smoothly; the screen warms as you move it. Arrow keys, Home and End work too.
- **Clearer panel:** errors and explanations appear inside the panel instead of a window that closed it; unavailable buttons are dimmed and say why; a slow update check no longer freezes the panel; pages keep their scroll position; driver roll back, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth restart and removing an app profile ask for a second click.
- **Fixes:** charge limits come back after sleep during Smart charging or "Charge to 100% once"; the microphone LED keeps working; app profiles and Quiet nights always restore the previous mode; reminders and battery notices arrive as Windows notifications when on-screen notices are off; the battery tile shows a dash when its reading is old and the charger's wattage on AC; switches stay visible with a grey Windows accent; clearer wording everywhere.
- **Stricter driver checks:** a driver package without HONOR's checksum must be signed by HONOR; revoked certificates are refused; BIOS updates re-check the charger right before flashing.
- When GitHub limits update checks from your address (common with a VPN), GrandHonor says so and when to try again.
