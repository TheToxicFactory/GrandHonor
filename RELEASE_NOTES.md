# GrandHonor 2.5.2

- **Display:** colour profiles Vivid, sRGB (accurate colours for the web and photos) and P3. Eye comfort warmth is now a slider. **Eye breaks** remind you every 20–60 minutes to look into the distance.
- **Battery:** **Charge to 100% once** before a trip, then the limit comes back. Notices when charging stops at the limit, at 20 % left, when the battery passes 50 °C and after sleep on battery ("Sleep 8 h: −4%"). Battery health shows how capacity changed over the months.
- **Power:** **Quiet nights** (Efficiency mode between two hours) and **App profiles** (Performance and the highest refresh rate while a chosen program runs).
- **Keys:** choose what the Copilot key and the YOYO key (F11) do: open GrandHonor, screenshot, microphone, power mode, Eye comfort or a program.
- **Tray:** right-click the icon for a quick menu.
- **Drivers → Device check:** devices with errors (restart or roll back the driver), SSD health, restart Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, a system report for support and the event log.
- HONOR OSD is turned off automatically, so notices never appear twice; the switch is gone.
- The CPU power limit is removed: it could not hold the limit reliably. A lowered processor setting is put back automatically.
